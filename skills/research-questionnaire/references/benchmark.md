# Benchmark against professional deliverables

When the user requests reference-level quality, read the actual questionnaire, quota sheet, appendices and programming notes. Build a comparison of business decisions, metrics, questions, answer lists, routing, variables and analysis bases. Do not use question or page count as a quality score.

Cover the modules needed by this study: eligibility, unprompted awareness, competitive funnels, brand perceptions and barriers, a recent purchase, channel touchpoints, overall experience/NPS, first versus repeat purchase, gifting or other occasions, segmentation constructs, stimuli and geography. An illustrative subset must not be presented as the complete instrument.

- Keep unaided awareness before cues. Past purchase must not be filtered through future consideration. Separate visits, purchases and payment channels; a cross-channel purchase/visit ratio is not store conversion.
- Localize brands, regions, currencies, tax treatment and language. Preserve raw state/province codes before regional grouping; do not copy another project's income or age exclusions.
- Ask about experience before rating touchpoints. Separate NPS zero from missing. Limit deep brand evaluation to known, selected brands and preserve selection probabilities.
- Provide full wording, all answer codes, eligibility, carry-forward, randomization, exclusivity, missing behavior and checks. Prose is not executed platform logic.
- Bind questionnaire, codebook, coding frames, stimuli and analysis definitions to the same version. Distinguish base, booster, experiencer and experiment samples.
- Measure burden in cognitive testing and pilot timing. Splitting modules requires revised sample bases; deleting decision-critical content merely to hit a duration target is not sufficient.

Deliver a readable master, programming acceptance checklist, matching codebook and analysis map. Follow the host's current Office authoring skill. Compiler exports are a baseline, not a professional-layout ceiling. Record business review, structural tests, localization and pilot status separately; none certifies equivalence to an institution.
