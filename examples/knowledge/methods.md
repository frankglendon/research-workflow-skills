# Original demonstration knowledge

This small library is authored for the public demo. It contains no company assets, client cases, empirical market findings or proprietary framework reproductions.

## Comparable price samples
For a price comparison, retain item, specification, country, currency, date, tax, promotion and availability. Compare matched products where possible. A few visible items describe a sample, not the market average. Keep unknown fields explicit.

## Country comparison
Compare countries only when definitions, periods, populations and units match. Keep local competitors and fulfillment conditions visible. A regional aggregate cannot be assigned to individual countries without further evidence.

## Competitive mechanism
Compare the same purchase task, product, channel, experience and observable outcome across competitors. Distinguish a disclosed action from evidence that the action caused success. Record resource requirements before recommending imitation.

## Opportunity judgment
Link each proposed opportunity to demand evidence, an unmet need and the client's capability. State alternative explanations and prerequisites. A qualitative priority does not require fabricated numeric scores.

## Price comparison page
Layout recipe: a conclusion title above a table of sampled items, specification, local price and conditions; an interpretation beside the table; source references below. Do not paste historical prices into a new study.

## Country comparison page
Layout recipe: countries as columns, consistent decision dimensions as rows, a separate row for missing evidence, and a final implication tied to an actual difference. This is an original text recipe, not a slide asset.

## Competitive action page
Layout recipe: competitor, observable action, outcome evidence and transfer conditions in parallel columns. Add a short conclusion supported by the comparison. Leave unsupported outcomes unfilled.

## Opportunity page
Layout recipe: list candidate opportunities with evidence, uncertainty and action prerequisites. A matrix needs clearly defined axes; use qualitative categories if measurement is unavailable.
